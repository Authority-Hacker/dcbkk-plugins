# Changelog

## 1.2.2 - 2026-09-24

### What changed

- Clarified when to use Content Humanizer for natural-sounding edits or a pattern-only review, with separate short and long marketplace descriptions.

### Why it matters

The descriptions now name common requests and boundaries so the assistant has clearer guidance for selecting the skill. The editing workflow itself is unchanged.

### What you need to do

Update Content Humanizer to 1.2.2. Share an existing draft and say whether you want a natural-sounding edit or a pattern-only review.

## 1.2.1 - 2026-09-24

### What changed

- Edits now work from the complete draft and any voice sample, then compare the complete result against the original before delivery.
- File jobs produce one complete result per input and keep the original available for fidelity checks. Follow-up edits preserve the latest accepted version and return a complete revised file.
- The scanner remains available for ambiguous patterns and exact punctuation checks, but its suggestions do not dictate edits.

### Why it matters

You can humanize long pieces and related variants without losing claims, qualifications, quotations, links, tables, or your own voice. A shorter follow-up should tighten wording without silently cutting substantive details.

### What you need to do

Update Content Humanizer to 1.2.1, then give it the full draft, any related variants, and a voice sample if you have one. Review the complete result against the source before publishing.
