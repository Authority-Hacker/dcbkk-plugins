# Humanizer maintenance

Keep the Claude and Codex manifests at the same identity and version. The student runtime is `skills/humanizer/`; `scan.py` is its sole executable and reports candidates without editing files. Keep examples source-faithful and protect direct quotations, links, placeholders, supported facts, and voice when changing the workflow.

Use the product's evidence directory for tests and review records, not the installed `src/`. Historical evidence and the archived review stamp apply only to their original source hashes. New source bytes need fresh outcome evidence and review before release.
